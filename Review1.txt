PROJECT: Dead Man’s Ledger: The Money Engine v0.2

Goal:
Turn v0.1 from a functional geodata-backed foundation into a visually convincing, playable board-game prototype.

Do not push anything until explicitly approved.

Current status:
v0.1 successfully solved the hardest technical/art problem:

* real geodata-backed U.S. map
* city nodes projected from latitude/longitude
* routes drawn dynamically from data
* no Google Maps assets
* no AI-generated geography
* typecheck/build/audit/preview all pass
* title/how-to/map preview/gameplay/result/fired/high-score screens exist

Do not abandon this pipeline.
Do not return to AI-generated geography.
Do not hand-place cities by guesswork.

Important:
Keep the base geography data-driven.
Keep city nodes and routes drawn from `nodes.ts` and `routes.ts`.
Only edit node coordinates if a factual coordinate is wrong.
Do not remove important nodes.

Required v0.2 focus:

1. Make the gameplay screen feel like a premium board-game table.
2. Make the map much larger and more readable.
3. Make route selection/prep assignment clearer.
4. Improve event/result presentation.
5. Improve the old-west ledger/gold/parchment art direction.
6. Preserve all working v0.1 gameplay.

Critical layout fixes:

* The center map currently appears too small with too much empty dark space.
* Redesign the gameplay layout so the map dominates the screen.
* The map should fill most of the central panel while preserving the side panels.
* Avoid large empty letterboxed areas above/below the map.
* Keep the app usable at 1440x900.
* Ensure the preparation panel does not cut off buttons.
* If necessary, make the prep panel scroll internally with a styled ledger scrollbar.
* Make current station, reachable exits, selected route, and assigned prep visible at a glance.

Map visual polish:

* Keep state geometry accurate.
* Make state boundary lines much fainter or visually blended into the parchment.
* Add antique map texture, subtle shaded relief, light paper grain, and engraved-map feel.
* Preserve Great Lakes, Upper Peninsula, Gulf Coast, Iowa/Missouri river region, Texas coast, Rockies, Great Plains, and Appalachians accuracy.
* The map should feel like an old western board-game map, not a modern GIS panel.
* Do not bake city labels/routes into the base map.
* Add visual polish through CSS/SVG overlays and generated textures, not by repainting geography with AI.

Route overlay polish:

* Make rail routes, stage routes, branch routes, and express routes visually distinct.
* Improve line weight and contrast.
* Express routes should feel tempting and dangerous.
* Active exits from the current station should glow or pulse gently.
* Inactive routes should remain visible but subdued.
* Selected route should have a strong gold highlight.
* Add small route markers/badges for assigned prep:

  * scout
  * guards
  * repair crew
  * telegraph
  * decoy
  * insurance
  * delay
  * credit/borrowed funding if applicable
* Add hover tooltip or selected-route card showing route type, risk, payout, known intel, hidden/unknown status, and assigned prep.

City/node polish:

* Keep all 55 nodes unless there is a clear reason to add/adjust.
* Make labels more readable but avoid Northeast label pileup.
* Add label collision mitigation:

  * smaller labels in dense regions
  * offset labels
  * hide some labels until hover in dense areas if needed
* Current station should be unmistakable.
* Destination or reachable nodes should feel clickable.

Gameplay clarity:

* Preserve mouse-first controls.
* No keyboard gameplay required.
* Flow should remain:
  click route
  click prep action
  click route to assign prep
  dispatch
  watch route movement
  result card
  continue
* Add a clearer “Selected Route” review before dispatch.
* When prep is assigned, update the route card immediately and visibly.
* Scout and Telegraph should reveal different kinds of intel:

  * Scout: physical threats such as ambush, track cut, bridge damage, scout missing risk
  * Telegraph: official warnings such as weather, sabotage, inside leak, telegraph cut, false tips
* Add “Local Tip” as a low-cost or free occasional intel mechanic if easy:

  * low reliability, around 20 to 40 percent
  * can reveal a true hint, false hint, or rumor
  * should feel flavorful, not required

Economy/interest polish:

* Keep this simple in v0.2.
* Make Borrow Credit visibly tied to interest.
* Display current debt or pending interest if the player borrows.
* Interest should nibble at score/cash after a dispatch or journey.
* Do not build a full banking sim yet.
* The finance layer should support the route-risk game, not take it over.

Event/result polish:

* Current event card works but looks placeholder.
* Improve event vignettes so they feel like small board-game incident animations.
* Use lightweight SVG/CSS, not full video files.
* Add distinct visual treatments for:

  * clean delivery
  * ambush
  * defended ambush
  * track cut
  * repair crew success
  * repair crew ambushed
  * bad weather
  * bridge damage
  * telegraph warning
  * inside leak
  * decoy strongbox success
  * shipment failed
* Event card should clearly show:

  * route
  * hazard
  * what prep helped or failed
  * payout
  * score change
  * cash change
  * trust/reputation change
  * short flavorful result text

Animation polish:

* Improve route dispatch animation:

  * token moves along selected route
  * selected route glows while moving
  * small dust/smoke/spark trail if simple
  * event vignette appears near the route or in modal
* Add small generated WebAudio cues if easy:

  * dispatch
  * success
  * failure
  * fired
  * button click
* Audio must be generated in-browser or simple WebAudio. No copyrighted audio.

Journey/high-score polish:

* Make one “journey” feel like a defined run segment, such as 6 to 8 deliveries.
* Add a milestone modal when a journey is completed:
  “ROUTE LEGEND”
  “The currency arrived. The towns trusted the notes. The engine kept turning.”
* Carry remaining cash forward.
* Increase pressure slightly on the next journey.
* Preserve localStorage high scores.
* Make high-score screen more thematic, like a ledger page.

Title and identity:

* Improve title screen if needed.
* Add or polish the gold logo:
  “Dead Man’s Ledger: The Money Engine”
* Subtitle:
  “The money provided independence. The route was the engine.”
* Logo should be gold/strongbox/ledger inspired, but do not cover gameplay map during play.

Art direction:

* Premium HD digital board-game.
* Old western ledger desk.
* Brass, parchment, gold, dark wood, ink, telegraph slips.
* Not 8-bit.
* Not cartoon western.
* Not generic dark SaaS dashboard.
* Not a modern map app.
